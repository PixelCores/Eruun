# Code of Conduct

Eruun contributors and maintainers are expected to create a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith while still asking for evidence.
- Welcome people of different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Give actionable feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, attribution, and intellectual-property boundaries.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes, and de-escalate conflict.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, sustained disruption, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Submitting confidential material, credentials, or code without the right to share it.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation when necessary to protect the community.

Maintainers must recuse themselves from cases in which they have a conflict of interest. Deliberately false or retaliatory reports may themselves violate this policy.
